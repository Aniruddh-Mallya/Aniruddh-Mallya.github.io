About me

I am a software engineer and early-career researcher based in Limerick, Ireland. My research focuses on requirements engineering, software architecture and trustworthy large language model (LLM) systems, with a particular interest in how software can adapt reliably to human needs.

I completed my MSc in Software Engineering at the University of Limerick, where my thesis on LLM-assisted requirements engineering became two first-authored publications, in collaboration with Lero, the Research Ireland Centre for Software, UCD and the University of Padova. Before that, I spent two years at Siemens Healthineers building safety-critical medical imaging software.

Going forward, I want to research human-in-the-loop autonomous and self-adaptive systems.

Research & software projects

RITA

AI-assisted requirements engineering tool

  • Uses open-source LLMs to classify user feedback and generate structured software requirements.
  • Reproducible evaluation workflows with configurable models and structured outputs.
  • Syncs generated user stories to live projects through the Jira Cloud REST API.
GitHub Paper

ELLA

Research management platform

  • Full-stack platform for organising academic literature, built on OpenAlex.
  • Hexagonal (ports-and-adapters) architecture with interchangeable data backends.
  • Authentication, automated tests, Docker deployment, and CI with GitHub Actions.
